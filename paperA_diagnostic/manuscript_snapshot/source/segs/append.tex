\section{Supplementary Diagnostic Notes}\label{appendix}

This supplementary note is limited to diagnostic material. Closed-loop operation, application-specific load shifting, energy-performance comparisons, and equipment-facing claims are outside the scope of this study.

\subsection{Run inventory}

The final diagnostic run contains 144 annual Medium Office cases. Each case has 35{,}040 15-minute rows and 16{,}704 occupied rows. The zone-raw trace schema contains 45 raw environmental fields: air temperature, mean radiant temperature, and relative humidity for each of 15 conditioned zones.

\subsection{Diagnostic outputs}

The principal generated outputs are:
\begin{itemize}
    \item mean-tail summary tables and scatter figures;
    \item scalar PMV and expected-TSV comparison tables;
    \item zone-aggregation summary tables and figures;
    \item metabolic-spread summary tables and figures;
    \item trace-schema and EnergyPlus run-quality audits.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Scope note}

All analyses in this study are diagnostic. Probability states are recorded from fixed-reference simulations. They are not used to actuate setpoints or evaluate closed-loop HVAC performance.
